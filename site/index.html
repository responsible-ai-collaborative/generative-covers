Generative covers

Candidate cover images for incidents whose reports have no image of their own. A job generates one image per incident, stores it in Cloudinary as <folder>/incident-<number>, and this page lists everything that exists. Preview an image, then copy its URL or Cloudinary public ID to use it on a report.

    To use a cover on the AIID, paste the image URL into the report's image field in the incident editor; the database mirrors the file into its own Cloudinary account automatically. Source and job configuration: responsible-ai-collaborative/generative-covers.
